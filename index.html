This project introduces an AI tool developed for Maison Alaïa, designed to support creative research and exploration. We collected every runway look from Alaïa's archives up until October 2026, allowing the system to study the brand's evolving forms, materials, and silhouettes. From this foundation, the AI can generate new collections, guided by selected themes or moods. It becomes a space for experimentation where data meets design, and where Alaïa's identity continues to evolve through new creative interpretations.
